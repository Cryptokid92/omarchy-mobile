# omamobile-audio

The Pixel 7 Pro's speaker and microphones go through the AoC, the audio and
sensor DSP, whose ALSA card is `aoc-snd-card`. This package makes them a
normal PipeWire speaker ("Built-in Audio Speaker") and microphone ("Built-in
Audio Microphone"), and runs the two CS35L41 speaker amplifiers with Cirrus's
protection firmware and the phone's factory calibration, as stock Android does.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `omamobile-speaker-protection` + `.service` (system, **not enabled by the package**) | loads the amplifier driver (or, when it is already bound, rebinds both amplifiers: a chip reset, so `systemctl restart` recovers a stuck DSP), mutes both at once, holds them out of runtime suspend (hibernation breaks the stock firmware), checks the kernel's volume limits, the stage's ceilings, the firmware and the factory calibration (`persist:/audio/speaker.cal` via debugfs, read-only), loads the protection firmware, writes `CAL_*` into both DSPs and reads it back, routes the DAC through the DSP, checks health (held awake, DSP controls present, no amplifier error in the kernel log), and only then unmutes. Any failure leaves both muted; a hang marker keeps the driver alone on the next boot. `status`, `health`, `monitor` and `verify` read the DSPs' state (coil temperature, CSPL state and errors, heartbeat), never the calibration values; `impedance` (during playback) reads the coil impedance the firmware measures, as a ratio to the factory value; `feedback start|stop|status` runs the amplifiers' VMON/IMON stream to the AoC as stock does (AOC-PLAN C9d-7); `mute` mutes. `report` (read-only) prints what a session needs first: kernel, packages, stage and levels, settings, the stamp and what it allows, `speaker_protection`, bring-up files in `/etc/modprobe.d`, firmware, mode, the speaker PCM's format, the TX slots and the filter, then `status`. `verify --at-level [DIGITAL ANALOG]` is the gate for stage c9 (AOC-PLAN C9i): during one short burst both firmwares must sense the coil (vmon low 0), measure Z/CAL_R 0.97-1.05 and show a coil rise below 1 °C that falls after the burst; its stamp records the levels, and c9 refuses anything above them (`c9-probe` keeps c9's ceilings for the C9d research bursts with any stamp). `mode earpiece|speaker`: the top amplifier alone with its DSP bypassed, as stock's handset path, at 737/6 at most (657/6 by default), the bottom muted; or back to both through the DSP |
| `omamobile-speaker-mode@.service` (system, started on request) + polkit `50-omamobile-speaker-mode.rules` | runs `mode earpiece` or `mode speaker` while the protection service runs; the rule lets the active local user start those two instances and nothing else (the "Voice Call" verb's devices do) |
| `omamobile-speaker-watch.service` | started with it and bound to it: mutes both amplifiers on an amplifier error in the kernel log (CSPL error, failed wake, failed resume), an amplifier no longer held awake, a level above the ceilings (in stage c9, the stamp's levels), or, during playback, a DSP not running the protection firmware with the calibration applied; in earpiece mode it expects the top amplifier at the earpiece's ceilings and the bottom one muted, and mutes on anything else; mutes too when it stops. Writes stock's media delta at stream start; with `OMAMOBILE_SPK_FEEDBACK=1` runs the V/I feedback stream with every speaker stream |
| `udev.rules` | holds the amplifiers awake from the moment the driver binds |
| `/etc/omamobile/speaker.conf` | stage (media, the default at 737 / 10; c8 bring-up; c9 stock under verified protection), the amplifiers' levels (per amplifier too), the media delta switch (on by default when installed) |
| `modprobe.d/omamobile-audio.conf` | blacklists `snd_soc_cs35l41_spi`, so the card only comes up through the service |
| UCM2 `conf.d/aoc-snd-card`, `Google/cheetah/HiFi.conf` | HiFi verb: Speaker = AoC EP6 to TDM 0 (the amplifiers), Mic = AoC EP1 from the built-in microphones; PCMs found by id. Touches no amplifier control, so PipeWire uses software volume |
| UCM2 `Google/cheetah/VoiceCall.conf` | "Voice Call" verb, never the default: Earpiece (the lowest priority) and Speaker only ask the protection service for earpiece or speaker mode (`omamobile-speaker-mode@`), Mic is the built-in microphones. It names no PCM and sets none of the call routes: the AoC and the modem carry the call, and omamobile-sitd owns the voice mixers and PCMs 4 and 11 for the length of a call. Whether PipeWire and callaudiod can drive a verb without PCMs is open (AOC-PLAN C9i, Session 6) |
| UCM2 `conf.d/aoc-snd-card/google,tegu-aoc-snd-card.conf`, `Google/tegu/HiFi.conf` | the Pixel 9a's profile, found through the card's long name before the shared `aoc-snd-card.conf`: TDM 0 in stock's format, Speaker = EP6, Mic = EP1 from microphones 0 and 1 only (cheetah's list takes the 9a's AoC down), DC blocker and 22 dB; stock's amplifier gains (the owner's `tas2572RCA1.bin`, loaded at every playback start), without stock's processing or protection, so the WirePlumber rule caps the sink at about -10 dB |
| WirePlumber `52-omamobile-tegu-aoc.conf` | the Pixel 9a's node names, PCM settings and the speaker volume cap (`channelmix.max-volume = 0.562`, a -10 dB ceiling: the clamp applies to the master and the channel volumes, so the ceiling is the square) |
| PipeWire `60-omamobile-speaker-loudness.conf` | the speaker filter (a smart filter in front of the speaker sink only; `nofail`, so PipeWire starts without it if it can't load): an input matrix (stereo width, left/right swapped in landscape), optional bass management (the bass below a crossover, in mono, to the bottom speaker only) and bass harmonics (the bass the speakers can't play, heard through its harmonics), then per speaker a 4th-order 100 Hz high-pass, a +4 dB low shelf at 400 Hz, a -2 dB presence dip at 3.5 kHz, a treble shelf and a trim, and a look-ahead limiter per speaker at -3 dBFS with no gain. Stock shapes the speaker sound on the AoC (Waves), which we don't send yet; without shaping the phone sounds thin. Needs PipeWire's builtin `abs` (1.4 or later) |
| `omamobile-speaker-eq` (as the desktop user) | the filter's values while playing: `show` (and whether the filter is in the path), `set bass=3 dip=-3 trim_top=-2 bassmono=1 harm=0.5 drive=2 ...`, `preset default|flat|waves`, `save` (a copy under `~/.config/pipewire/pipewire.conf.d/` that replaces the package's at the next PipeWire start), `reset`, `orient` / `follow` (left and right from the phone's orientation); `measure left|right` records stepped tones with the phone's microphone (or plays them for another microphone, `--play-only`) and prints the speaker's response and harmonics (`lib/speaker_response.py`, which also has `suggest` and `compare`) |
| `omamobile-speaker-orient.service` (user unit, **not enabled**) | runs `omamobile-speaker-eq follow`: the speakers' left and right follow the phone's orientation, as stock swaps them in landscape |
| WirePlumber `51-omamobile-aoc.conf` | no mmap and 4 × 20 ms periods for the AoC PCMs (as the stock HAL), software volume only, names for the card and its nodes; the call PCMs (4, EP5 playback, and 11, EP4 capture) are never PipeWire nodes |

The protection firmware and the per-speaker tunings are Google's and are not
shipped: `tools/extract-vendor-firmware` copies them from the user's own
factory image to `cirrus/cs35l41-dsp1-spk-prot-cheetah{.wmfw,-top.bin,-bottom.bin}`, and
stock's media deltas to `cirrus/cs35l41-cheetah-{top,bottom}-media-delta.txt`.
The calibration never leaves the phone.

Needs the AoC running (`omamobile-aoc.service` in omamobile-device-cheetah)
and a kernel with the AoC sound card and its speaker limits. Bring-up:
`device/cheetah/AOC-PLAN.md`, stages C8, C8f and C9. The kernel should also carry `cirrus,dsp-no-hibernate`
on both amplifiers (branch `omamobile-audio-amp`); the service holds them awake
either way.
