# Koi pond

A pixel-art koi pond in your theme's colours, with a lily pad. Tap the water to feed the koi: they turn and swim to the food, circle it, then drift home.

A built-in home-screen widget, ported from
[`simonschubert/koi-pond`](https://github.com/SimonSchubert/omarchy-widgets/tree/main/widgets/simonschubert/koi-pond)
by simonschubert, in [omarchy-widgets](https://github.com/SimonSchubert/omarchy-widgets),
the widget store of Simon Schubert's Omalauncher.

**Differences from the original:** the swimming is the original's (in `Pond.js`), but the pond moves a step once a second and only swims smoothly for a few seconds after a tap, to keep the home screen cheap. The koi are the text and a warm mix of the urgent colour, with urgent and text spots.

**Capabilities:** none.
