# Today

The date, and the weather where you are: located by IP, or the city you set.
Current temperature and conditions, the day's high and low, the next two
days and sunrise and sunset. Tap it for the details: the next 24 hours with
a temperature curve and rain chances, three days on one temperature scale,
wind, humidity, UV, pressure, visibility and the moon. Tap the header to
come back.

A built-in home-screen widget, ported from
[`simonschubert/today`](https://github.com/SimonSchubert/omarchy-widgets/tree/main/widgets/simonschubert/today)
by Simon Schubert, in [omarchy-widgets](https://github.com/SimonSchubert/omarchy-widgets),
the widget store of Simon Schubert's Omalauncher.

**Differences from the original:** The details open inside the card instead
of a full screen, and the city is a setting (empty means located by IP)
instead of a text field there. Times follow the system's 12/24-hour setting.
Colours come from the Omarchy theme: the accent stands in for the original's
cyan, yellow and blue.

**Capabilities:** `network` (wttr.in; yr.no opens in the browser).
